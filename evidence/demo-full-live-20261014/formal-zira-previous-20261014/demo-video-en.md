# StudyFlow — Formal English Demo Script

[Current formal MP4](../evidence/demo-live-20261014/StudyFlow_Demo_EN.mp4) · [Full acceptance and recordings](../evidence/demo-full-live-20261014/README.md)

Runtime: **4 minutes 50 seconds**. Actual footage of the updated interface; 12 completed live-AI turns; all financial transactions simulated. Forty-six short narration lines are paired with recorded states, with English captions timed from 16 kHz SAPI word events. A continuous PCM narration track is encoded once to prevent cumulative AAC padding. Feature chapters group the chronological raw recordings for a concise presentation.
