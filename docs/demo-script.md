# StudyFlow Current Demo Flow (2026-10-15)

This page is kept in sync with the [unified frontend/backend description](student-loan-contract.md). All loans, admission letters, balances and payments are simulated.

## Full flow after sign-in

1. In the chat, look up the full-program total cost of schools, compare schools, and choose a school and program length.
2. Open "My plan" and enter the loan amount directly. There is no available-funds input, no funding-gap calculation, and no "no loan needed" confirmation option.
3. Choose the product, term and repayment method, then click "Estimate repayments". Check principal, interest, fees, monthly payment and total repayment; after changing the amount you must estimate again.
4. Click "Confirm plan and apply", review the confirmation page and submit. The application automatically attaches a simulated admission letter; underwriting checks that the amount does not exceed the full-program cost, and fees do not raise the loan cap.
5. "To-dos and bills" and "Wallet & payments" open at this point. Before the application is submitted, wallet, FX and remittance requests in the chat are refused with a prompt to submit the loan application first (server-side `APPLICATION_REQUIRED`).
6. Mark the remaining documents complete, then explicitly confirm signing. The admission letter is completed automatically and cannot be unticked manually; there is no enrollment-confirmation step.
7. Signing creates a per-semester payment schedule to the school and a real simulated loan account. Review the school bills not covered by the loan and, if needed, pay them from the simulated wallet.
8. In "To-dos and bills", advance the demo date to the repayment date, return to "My plan" to confirm the repayment and complete password verification as the policy requires. Check the installment paid, the remaining balance and the ledger.

The actual end-to-end regression is `frontend/tests/e2e/integration/plan.spec.mjs`. It uses the real Django/PostgreSQL API, an isolated account and the rule-based planner, and covers the automatic admission letter, signing, the school payment schedule and repayment; it does not require resetting the development database.

## Read-only preview without a backend

Open `/?tour=1` for the 8-stop tour: price lookup, comparison, loan estimate, application and documents, to-dos and bills, wallet, payment, and an explanation of the repayment flow. The tour has two stages, `plan` (consultation) and `prepare` (application submitted). Old `stage=school` links are mapped to `prepare` for compatibility.

- Consultation and loan estimate: `/?plan=preview&tool=funding`
- Application submitted and automatic letter: `/?plan=preview&stage=prepare&tool=funding`
- To-dos and bills: `/?plan=preview&stage=prepare&tool=life`
- Wallet and payments: `/?plan=preview&stage=prepare&tool=wallet`

The preview blocks writes and never signs or repays; the last stop explains the repayment steps. Both the Chinese and English recorded data are regenerated by the unified API recorder.
